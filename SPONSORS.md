# Support MailFlow

MailFlow is developed independently in spare time and remains open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0), regardless of sponsorship.

Sponsorship is completely optional. It helps cover the time and infrastructure required to keep the project moving forward, including:

- development and maintenance
- CI and test infrastructure
- compatibility testing with local and hosted LLMs
- security testing and hardening
- domains, hosting, and development tooling
- documentation and localization

## Suggested sponsorship levels

These are intentionally simple and do not unlock proprietary features or contractual support.

- **€3 / month — Supporter**: helps with recurring project costs
- **€5 / month — MailFlow Supporter**: supports ongoing maintenance and testing
- **€10 / month — Project Backer**: helps fund continued feature development
- **€25 / month — Major Supporter**: provides substantial recurring support
- **Custom amount**: for individuals or organizations that want to contribute more

One-time sponsorships are also welcome where supported by the sponsorship platform.

## What sponsorship does not buy

Sponsorship is voluntary support for the open-source project. It does not create a support contract, service-level agreement, guaranteed feature delivery, priority bug fixing, proprietary license, or ownership interest in the project.

MailFlow remains available under AGPL-3.0 independently of whether a user or organization sponsors development.

## Recognition

Sponsors may be acknowledged publicly in this file or elsewhere in the project if they explicitly want public recognition. Anonymous sponsorship is equally welcome.

Organizations that depend on MailFlow in production are especially encouraged to support its continued independent development.

## Transparency

Project sponsorship income should be treated as income according to the applicable tax rules of the recipient. This repository does not issue donation receipts or represent sponsorship as a tax-deductible charitable donation.
